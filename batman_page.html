Google Batman Easter Egg

Quick Facts

Summary

Try the Easter Egg

The Original Easter Egg

How It Started

Google launched the Batman Search Easter egg on February 23, 2022, in the run-up to The Batman. Searches like Bruce Wayne, Gotham City, and Bat-Signal brought up a small yellow Bat-Signal icon beside the results.

What It Did

Click the icon and the page turns into a stormy Gotham night. Clouds roll in, thunder sounds, the Bat-Signal glows overhead, and a grappling sound leads into Batman swinging across the screen.

Impact and Reach

Movie-promo coverage and social clips kept coming back to the Bat-Signal because the payoff was immediate. A tiny trigger could turn an ordinary results page into a full Gotham scene in a few seconds.

Its Discontinuation

Google removed the official promo in February 2023, so the live version no longer appears in Search.

The Restored Experience

What’s Different Here

This restoration keeps the glowing Bat-Signal, storm clouds, thunder, and Batman's swing intact, while smoothing the experience for current browsers and phones.

The Easter Egg Experience

Open the page, find the Bat-Signal, and tap it to start the Gotham storm. Your page darkens, thunder rolls, and Batman swings across the page before the scene settles back and leaves the signal ready for another tap.

How to Try It

  1. Click the button above to open the restored page.
  2. Find the glowing Bat-Signal beside the results.
  3. Tap it to bring in the stormy Gotham night.
  4. Watch the thunder, clouds, and Batman swinging across the screen.
  5. When you want to watch it again, tap the Bat-Signal one more time.

The original effect worked because it was quick and legible: one small trigger, one strong mood change, one unmistakable hero silhouette. This restoration keeps that compact rhythm so the moment still lands cleanly on modern screens.

Final Thoughts

The official promo is gone, but this restored version keeps the Bat-Signal, the storm, and Batman swinging ready whenever you want another Gotham moment.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What was the Google Batman Easter egg?

It was an official Google Search Easter egg tied to The Batman in early 2022. Search for Batman-related terms and a small yellow Bat-Signal appeared beside the results.

Clicking it turned the page into a stormy Gotham night, complete with thunder, clouds, and Batman swinging across the screen.

Google removed the official promo in February 2023, but this page restores the short sequence people remember.

How do I trigger or watch it again?

In the original rollout, search for Bruce Wayne, Gotham City, Bat-Signal, or Batman to surface the yellow signal icon, then click it to start the scene.

Here, just open the restored page and tap the Bat-Signal. When the scene settles, tap the same signal again to watch the Gotham beat one more time.

Is this a full Batman game?

No. It is a short Search Easter egg built around a single Bat-Signal trigger and one quick Gotham scene.

The page is about the mood and the swing across the screen, not a score, level, or long play loop.
