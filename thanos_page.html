Try the Easter Egg

The Original Easter Egg

How It Started

In April 2019, to coincide with the release of “Avengers: Endgame,” Google hid a playful surprise in Search. Look up “Thanos,” and a golden Infinity Gauntlet appears in the knowledge panel — just waiting to be clicked.

What It Did

Tap the gauntlet and the snap fires. Half of the results dissolve into dust with a cinematic animation and sound, and the results counter drops by roughly 50%. Tap the gauntlet again and the vanished results reassemble — like rewinding time with the Time Stone.

Impact and Reach

The effect spread quickly across social media and became one of Google’s most talked-about Easter eggs. The little touches — dusting particles, the shifting counter, the audio cue — made the gag feel smart, polished, and memorable.

Its Discontinuation

As with many movie tie-ins, Google retired the Thanos Snap in 2020. Fans missed it — so we restored the experience here, so you can keep snapping (and unsnapping) today.

The Restored Experience

What’s Different Here

Our restoration matches the original look and feel: the gauntlet glows, the snap lands cleanly, and results dust away with satisfying timing. We’ve also tuned performance so it runs smoothly on modern browsers and devices — without losing that authentic vibe.

The Easter Egg Experience

Click the button above to wield the gauntlet. You’ll see it beside “Thanos” in the results — one tap snaps, half the items dust out, and the counter updates before a second tap brings everything back.

How to Try It

  1. Click the button above.
  2. Find the golden Infinity Gauntlet in the knowledge panel.
  3. Click the gauntlet to trigger the snap.
  4. Watch half the results disintegrate into dust.
  5. See the results count drop by 50%.
  6. Hear the unmistakable snap sound.
  7. Click again to reverse the snap and restore the page.

We preserve the cinematic feel with carefully timed particle effects and synced audio, and we’ve optimized the experience for cross-platform performance. Whether you’re on desktop or mobile, the Infinity Stones are ready for action.

Final Thoughts

The Thanos Snap blended Marvel’s big-screen moment with everyday search in a way people still talk about. Google’s original may be gone, but our faithful restoration keeps the fun alive. Try the gauntlet now, then explore more of our revived pop-culture Easter eggs!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What was the Google Thanos Snap Easter egg?

The Google Thanos Snap Easter egg was a 2019 Google Search Easter egg tied to Avengers: Endgame. When people searched for “Thanos,” Google showed an Infinity Gauntlet in the results.

Clicking it played the snap, turned part of the results page into drifting dust, and lowered the result count. A second click reversed the effect and restored the page.

How do I use the restored Thanos Easter egg?

Click the button above to enter the restored search page, then click the golden Infinity Gauntlet beside the results.

The first click starts the snap and dust effect. The second click triggers the Time Stone-style restore, bringing the vanished results back into view.
